Board composition
Public companies only (s.197 does not apply to private companies). Overall ceiling 11% of net profit; one MD/WTD/manager 5%, all of them together 10%; non-executive directors 1% (if there is an MD/WTD/manager) or 3% (if not). Sitting fees are outside these limits (max ₹1 lakh per meeting, Rule 4). Limits can be exceeded with a special resolution (and prior approval of the bank/financial institution/secured creditors if in default). Where profits are inadequate, Schedule V Part II Section II applies: effective capital below ₹5 crore — ₹60 lakh a year; ₹5–100 crore — ₹84 lakh; ₹100–250 crore — ₹120 lakh; above ₹250 crore — ₹120 lakh plus 0.01% of the excess; doubled with a special resolution.
