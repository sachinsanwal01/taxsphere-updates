Find Meesho's GSTIN for your state in GSTR-2A → TCS Credit Received on the GST portal, or on your Meesho commission invoice.

Import Meesho report

  1. Meesho Supplier Panel → Payments → Download → GST Report.
  2. Extract the ZIP. You get tcs_sales.xlsx and tcs_sales_return.xlsx.
  3. Upload both files below (XLSX or CSV).
📂 Tap to choose filestcs_sales / tcs_sales_return
In the TaxSphere app the file picker may not open. If it doesn't, use Paste from Excel below.

    Paste from Excel works in app

    1. Open tcs_sales.xlsx in Excel, Google Sheets or WPS on your phone.
    2. Select all cells, including the header row, and copy.
    3. Paste below, pick Sales or Returns, and tap Import. Repeat for tcs_sales_return.

    Add entry manually

    Input tax credit (ITC) from GSTR-2B

    Enter eligible ITC from your purchase bills and Meesho commission / shipping invoices as shown in GSTR-2B.

    Export & file

    CSVs follow the GST Offline Tool section layout, so you can import or copy them while filing.

    Filing checklist

    1. GSTR-1 (11th monthly / 13th quarterly): fill Table 7 B2CS state-wise with Meesho GSTIN, Table 12 HSN (B2C tab), Table 14 supplies through ECO, and Table 13 documents issued.
    2. Check GSTR-2A → TCS Credit Received and accept Meesho's TCS entry.
    3. GSTR-3B (20th / 22nd–24th): Table 3.1(a), Table 3.2 state-wise inter-state B2C, Table 4 ITC.
    4. Pay balance using TCS cash ledger, then cash.
    This tool prepares working figures. Verify them against your GSTR-2A/2B and portal auto-fill before submitting, and consult a GST practitioner if unsure. Figures are estimates, not tax advice.
