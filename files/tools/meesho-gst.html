Import Meesho report

  1. Meesho Supplier Panel → Payments → Download → GST Report.
  2. Extract the ZIP. You get tcs_sales.xlsx and tcs_sales_return.xlsx.
  3. Upload both files below (XLSX or CSV), or the filled TaxSphere template.

    Filing checklist

    1. GSTR-1 (11th monthly / 13th after the quarter): upload the GSTR-1 JSON (Tables 7, 12, 13 and 14) via Prepare Offline, check the summary and file.
    2. Found a mistake after filing GSTR-1? File GSTR-1A for the same period before GSTR-3B. GSTR-3B Tables 3.1/3.2 can't be edited any more.
    3. Check GSTR-2A → TCS credit received, compare it in the GSTR-3B step (Match with Meesho) and accept Meesho's TCS entry.
    4. GSTR-3B (20th monthly / 22nd or 24th quarterly): Table 4 ITC from GSTR-2B (Meesho commission & shipping invoices), then pay using the TCS in your cash ledger first.
    5. Returns older than 3 years from their due date can no longer be filed, so don't leave any month pending.
