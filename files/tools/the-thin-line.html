Company tax planning guide

Every lever here is Zone 1 or Zone 2 when it rests on a real business reason and a paper trail. Each card shows what it saves, the conditions, the documents to keep, and where it tips into GAAR or evasion territory.

Step 1Normal regime or the 22% concessional regime?

The first decision every company makes

Section 200 of the 2025 Act (old s.115BAA) offers 22% plus a flat 10% surcharge and 4% cess, an effective 25.168%, in exchange for giving up most incentives. The normal regime keeps incentives but, from tax year 2026-27, MAT at 14% (s.206) becomes a final tax and no MAT credit can be used. Opting in is irrevocable.
