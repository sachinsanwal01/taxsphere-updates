Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

TaxSphere ("the app") is made by Sachin Sanwal. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data kept on your device

Your study progress, notes, quiz scores, calculator inputs and GST data are saved on your own device. They are not sent anywhere unless you use cloud backup.

Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them only to identify your account for cloud backup and purchases. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

Cloud backup

When you tap Back up (or turn on daily backup), a copy of the app data on your device is stored on our server (Cloudflare) under your account so you can restore it on another device. We keep your latest backup and one older copy. You can ask us to delete your account and backups at any time.

Usage count

Once a day, when the app opens with internet, it sends a random install number, the app version and the platform (Android, Windows or web). This only counts how many people use TaxSphere. It contains no name, email, phone number or any of your data.

Payments

Paid tools are bought by UPI. We store the item, amount and the UPI reference (UTR) you enter so we can verify the payment. We never see or store your bank, card or UPI PIN details.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone, except service providers that run the app (Cloudflare for the server, GitHub for app updates) or where the law requires it.

Deleting your data

Email sachintaxsphere@gmail.com from your signed-in Gmail address to delete your account, backups and purchase records.
